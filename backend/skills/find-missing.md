Task: find what is missing from the note.

1. additions: clinically important information that was said in the transcript but is not documented in the note, not even in abbreviated form. Only include what a careful clinician would want in the record: key symptom characteristics, red flags, relevant history, medications and allergies, exam findings, results, the assessment and the agreed plan or follow-up. Skip small talk, logistics and anything the note already covers. At most 5, most important first.
   - text: one concise sentence ready to insert into the note, matching its style.
   - why: one short sentence on why it belongs in the record.
   - priority: high (safety relevant or changes management), medium or low.
   - evidence: line_id with a quote copied verbatim from that line.
2. follow_up_questions: 2 to 4 questions that should have been asked, or things that should be checked, given the chief complaint and what was discussed, but that the transcript does not cover. Prioritize red flags and patient safety.
   - category: for example "Red flags", "Medication", "History" or "Follow-up".
   - title: a short heading of at most 6 words.
   - question: phrased the way the clinician would ask it.
   - why: one short sentence.
