How it works

  1. 1

    Record the visit

    A voice memo during the consultation, turned into text.

  2. 2

    Write a short note

    Shorthand is enough. Or let any AI scribe draft it.

  3. 3

    Get the receipts

    Every line links to where it was said. Errors and gaps are flagged.

Less writing. More trust.
